Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They're commonly used to make websites work properly, remember preferences, or gather usage information.

2. Cookies on this website

We keep cookie use on this website to a minimum. We do not currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Some essential technical cookies or similar data may be set automatically by services this website relies on to function:

  • Netlify (our hosting and form provider): may set essential cookies required for our website hosting and form submission to work correctly.
  • Google Fonts and Google Maps: embedded on some pages to display our typefaces and location maps. Loading these may involve your browser making a request to Google's servers, which is subject to Google's own cookie and privacy practices.

3. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies from specific sites or all sites, though doing so may affect how some parts of this website display or function (for example, embedded maps). For general guidance on managing cookies, visit aboutcookies.org.

4. Changes to this policy

If we introduce analytics or other cookie-based tools in future, we will update this policy to reflect that before doing so.

5. Contact us

Questions about our use of cookies? See our Company Information page for contact details.
